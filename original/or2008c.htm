Southern English Setter Society Open show Stakes Classes

One general comment, I was concerned by the prevalence of constructional faults in both front and back ends. Please watch these when breeding. On the plus side all dogs were clean and well presented.

Michael Winch Yearling Memorial Award (8, 1ab)

Two nice bitches headed this class

1st Cozen's Larrenie Bony Lass at Earldoms. 19 months O/B, Good length of neck, textbook shoulders. Deep well sprung ribs, short coupled and well set on tail, in good coat. Moved soundly with correct tail action.

2nd Morgan, Bott & Petherick's Quensha Little Jeanie, feminine, promising O/B. Preferred her head and bone, but not the shoulders of 1. lovely body and topline. Giving a lot away in maturity but her sound movement gave her place here. Well handled.

3rd Richard's Decoverly Sweet Caroline with Rubuscott

Lesley Allan-Scott Open Memorial Award (6,2ab)

1st Day's Mandyset Ready Steady Go To Deoverly. Won here with a bit to spare. Mature O/B, wish there was more of her to enjoy, but she is so well balanced. Presents a lovely shape when stood. Soft expression, good length of neck, beautiful shoulders, and straight front, deep body, short coupled, good width across the thigh. Moves sound and true.

2nd Slack's Ravensett Dark Fire, upstanding Dark Blue, beautiful head with the darkest of eyes giving a soft expression, good body, and sound backend. Just not the forehand or overall balance of 1.

3rd Montgomery's Carbine Istabraq of Radlake
