OPEN SHOW 21th July 2019.

My thanks to the committee for the invitation to judge & for their warm welcome & hospitality. My thanks must also go to my 2 stewards who kept the ring running smoothly.

BIS was Oliver's Bramstorm Oberon, RBIS Day's Mandyset Oh Boy at Decoverly, BV Jolley's Wansleydale Tribute to Jollymore & BP Dennis, Morgan & Harris' Gemsett Ice Cold In Alex at Mariglen.

On the whole mouths were good, but there were a worrying number of gay tails, spoiling the elegant outline on the move. I also found it concerning, that well angulated shoulders were hard to find, especially in the dogs.

21.07.2019 Southern English Setter Society Open Show - Memorial Awards

Thank you to the Society for inviting me to judge at the SESS Open Show 2019. I was made very welcome and had a lovely day. Thank you also to those who entered the Memorial Classes. Presentation was exceptional and it was a privilege to judge your dogs.

Michael Winch Special Yearling Memorial Award (7,2)

1. Sharples' Kanietter Seraphina. Well-constructed 8 month orange bitch with lean head, low ears, dark eyes and the softest expression. Clean neck into well laid shoulders with good return of upper arm. Deep chest, decent ribs, short coupling and level topline. Excellent bend of stifle to balance her front and a well set tail. Didn't give away her age on the move, covering the distance with drive and style.

2. Worthy's Tattersett Big Orange. 21 month orange dog in excellent coat and condition. Balanced head, good eyes and a kind expression. Decent neck flowing into good shoulders and a strong topline. Deep in chest, tight elbows and nicely bodied. Strong on the move, with lovely width to the rear. Not quite the angles of 1, but a very promising boy.

3. Taylor & Ateyo's Samelen Comeback Special. Gentle blue boy who just needs time to develop.

Lesley Allan-Scott Open Memorial Award (6,2)

1. Thompsett's Radbrooks Sloop John B. Handsome tri dog of correct proportions. Chiselled head with low ears and expressive dark eyes. Long neck into nicely laid shoulders with good return of upper arm. Short coupling, standing well at the rear with excellent bend of stifle. Elegant on the move, elbows tucked in, lovely forward reach and using his tail well. Would like a little more body and coat to complete the picture.

2. Taylor & Ateyo's Samelen Golden Taffeta. Well-presented orange bitch, in good coat and condition. Strong head with dark eyes, a clean, elegant neck and level topline. Nicely balanced angles to her front and rear construction. Excellent spring of rib and good bone throughout. Not quite as true as 1 on the move today.

3. Stirk's Mariglen Perfect Day For Grakar ShCM. Lovely blue girl, so enthusiastic she gave her handler a hard time in this class.

Eleanor Bryant
