Southern English Setter Society-Bitches

17th November 2013

I would like to thank the society giving me the opportunity to judge at their Championship Show. I was also very grateful to receive the good entry that I did.

One thing I must say was quite noticeable was just how many exhibits had light eyes which are really taking away the softness of the expression that our breed should have. On another negative, and this was only really in the younger classes was an awful lot of high tail actions. I would forgive this more in a male but there is no reason for bitch's to be doing it!

On the positive mouths seemed not too bad and temperaments were what is expected of our breed.

Bitches

Southern English Setter Championship show 17th November 2013 held at Tadley, Hampshire.

I was somewhat surprised and disappointed at how much the majority of the English Setters have changed and unfortunately not for the better. The standard asks for bright, mild and expressive oval eye. I saw plenty of dogs with round protruding eyes which totally changes the soft melting expression we used to expect from the breed. Feet also seem to be a problem, again very few have the well padded tight feet with close well arched toes. And shoulders, well I could probably count on one hand the dogs that actually had the correct layback of shoulder with good return of upper arm. Upright shoulders and short upper arm seem to be the order of the day and this does hinder the movement, a dog cannot reach in front with a short upper arm and incorrect shoulders. On the upside I was pleased with my winners there are nice examples about so please think carefully before breeding.
