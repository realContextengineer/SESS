SOUTHERN ENGLISH SETTER SOCIETY - OPEN SHOW - 19 July 2015

I would like to thank the Committee and Exhibitors for giving me this opportunity to assess a lovely selection of quality dogs on a glorious sunny day set in beautiful surroundings at Chieveley in the Berkshire countryside.

Michael Winch Yearling Memorial Award (3)

1. Williams Bournehouse Spring Violet, 17 month B/B young lady, feminine well-proportioned head with good eye and soft expression, balanced in skull and length of muzzle with defined stop and low set ears. Good neck and shoulders with well-sprung ribs for age, level topline and well-angulated hindquarters. She was presented in good coat and condition and moved around the ring with plenty of drive.

Lesley Allan-Scott Open Memorial Award (11)

1.Trigwell & Weller's Hannahdene Danny Boy With Juldeane JW, most handsome O/B boy almost 2 ½ years old who exudes maleness, and is so well-proportioned throughout. Has a balanced head with square muzzle and well-defined stop, with soft expression and dark eye. Good reach of neck leading into well-placed shoulders, good depth to chest with good spring of ribs carried well back, level topline with correct tailset. He has strong bone, standing on good feet and with powerful well-angulated quarters which he used to cover the ground in style. Shown in good coat and condition.

2. Harris's Gemsett Ultra Violet, 3 year old B/B lady presenting a lovely outline when stood with everything in proportion. She has a really attractive head with dark eye and soft feminine expression and with low set ears. Long reach of neck through well-set shoulders, deep chest with a well sprung ribcage, she has balanced quarters with good angulation and bend of stifle. Beautifully presented and in good coat and condition, moved well.
