SOUTHERN ENGLISH SETTER CHAMPIONSHIP SHOW

Sunday 23 November 2008 at Tidworth Leisure Centre

MINOR PUPPY, BITCH 2(1). 1st LEWIS Mr K.E. & Mrs G.M. KEGIL GOLDEN DELICIOUS Just six months old, well balanced baby, sweet head and expression, dark eye, correct mouth, in lovely condition, moved very soundly for her age. Very promising puppy.

PUPPY, BITCH 6. 1stBAKER Mrs G. MANDYSET POP TART. 10mth old dark tri, full of energy, lovely head and expression, good shoulders, top line and hind quarters, never stopped wagging her tail showing true setter spirit. Very pleased she was awarded best puppy in show. 2nd PITTS Mr S. WANDSFELL TISWAS. Another quality puppy, not quite as forward as 1, but nevertheless deserving her 2nd place. Stricking colour, very sound, will watch her progress.

VETERAN, BITCH 6(3) 1ST. TUCKER Mrs C. J. WALSHAW LET'S PARTY. Very nice class of oldies but at 9yrs old this was my winner. Still moving well and holding her outline, lovely coat and condition. Best veteran in show. 2nd DUNKS Mr & Mrs P. J. SAMELEN STAR BRIGHT. A favourite of mine, lovely bitch, also very fit and enjoying her day, but not quite the verve of 1.

JUNIOR, BITCH 4(3). 1ST BAKER Mrs G. MANDYSET WANNA BE A ROCK STAR. Although alone, deserving of a 1st. Very excitable, but well made and balanced.

YEARLING, BITCH 8(6). 1ST LITTLECHILD Mrs S. & DAVIES Mrs RAVENSETT DARK SECRETS. Blue bitch, excelled in neck and shoulders, lovely straight top line and hind quarters, moved very well with lovely tail action. 2nd DAVIS Mrs Y. CULVERWELL CALYPSO OF MERLEYCOPSE Bigger type of bitch, carrying much more coat than 1 but a little overweight and loose in her movement.

MAIDEN, BITCH 3(2). 1ST NEATH Mrs J. ALBADORA MISS QUIZADORA Placed vhc in puppy, very raw, moved much better in this class but needs time.

NOVICE, BITCH 5(4). 1ST BAKER Mrs G. MANDYSET WANNA BE A ROCK STAR. 1ST in Junior Bitch

TYRO, BITCH 2(2).

GRADUATE, BITCH 3 1ST . DAY Mrs J. DECOVERLY MAMMA MIA. Very nice orange bitch, lovely expression and good mouth, very short coupled straight front and short hocks. Lovely mover. 2nd HEWITT Mr & Mrs D. TATTERSETT DOUBLE ACTION Another well made bitch, very feminine, moved well, not quite the coat and condition of 1.

POST GRADUATE, BITCH 7(2). 1ST HARRIS Ms. L. Harris & DENISS Mrs J. BONDGATE EVENING STAR OVERMARIGLEN JW. Clearly the winner, this exquisite very well balanced tri bitch, with no exaggeration. Beautiful head, dark eye and the sort of soft expression I was looking for. Strong neck with good lay of shoulder. Straight front, good bend of stifle, lovely bone and well muscled, good depth and spring of rib. In the best of coats and condition, moved soundly and was thrilled to give her the CC. 2nd MARSHALL Miss D. A. CHEYNEYGATE BLESMA SOUL. Another sound bitch, finer than 1, not quite in the same coat and condition.

LIMIT, BITCH 12(2). 1ST GRIMSDELL Mr & Mrs A. TATTERSETT AIR OF ELEGANCE. Very feminine orange bitch, lovely head with dark eye and correct mouth. Looked a picture standing, she was also well muscled up which showed in her flowing movement. Unfortunately a little short of coat today and therefore reserve CC. 2nd DAY Mrs J. MANDYSET READY STEADY GO TO DECOVERLY. Another very nice bitch, a shade smaller in frame than 1. Well made, unfortunately really excitable which there her movement slightly.

OPEN, BITCH 11(3) 1ST LITTLECHILD Mrs S. SH. CH. RAVENSETT BIANCA. Very elegant feminine bitch. Lovely straight front, level top line, presented and showed extremely well, unfortunately also short of coat. 2nd MONTELL Mrs A. CULVERWELL SUMMER COLOURS. Tri bitch, slightly heavier type but well made, plenty of bone, in good coat and condition, moved extremely well.

SPECIAL BEGINNERS, BITCH 4(2). 1ST DAVIS Mrs Y. CULVERWELL CALYPSO OF MERLEYCOPSE. 2ND in Yearling. 2nd .GREENHAM Mr & Mrs T A Greenham LEWKINS WIDELOAD WINNIE AT FRIZZANTI. Orange bitch, dark eye, level topline, good bend of stifle and sound mover.
