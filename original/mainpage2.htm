A note from Steve Collier

Hi AllA quick update and a reminder to check out the KC ‘Find a Judge’ website. From January 2026 this will be the only official list of judges for all breeds and only judges listed on ’Find a Judge’ can be offered new judging contracts.

I would recommend that you become familiar with how the website works and how to review judges at the various Levels for English Setters.

Despite sending out several emails, there are still a lot of ‘breed specialist’ judges who haven’t registered at Levels 1 and 2, and we (all breed clubs) need to encourage them to register ASAP. When you look at Level 2 judges on ‘Find a Judge’ for ES, you will be surprised how many names are listed and how few breed specialists there are! I am constantly checking and questioning the KC about some of the names listed, which I have never heard of. Currently there are 3 names on Level 2 that I think should be at Level 1 and the KC are looking into it.

Please let me know if you have any questions or need help with navigating the ‘Find a Judge’ website?

Best Regards

Steve
