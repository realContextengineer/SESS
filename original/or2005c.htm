JUDGES REPORT - S.E.S.S. OPEN SHOW 17/07/05

Southern English Setter Society Open Show at Chieveley Village Hall on 17/07/05

I found the dogs were all clean and well presented, the weather was very hot which unfortunately influenced movement in many cases, the bitches coped far better than the dogs. I thoroughly enjoyed my day, although I wished I had more red cards as decisions were very close in a number of classes.
