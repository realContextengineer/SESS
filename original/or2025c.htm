Southern English Setter Open Show - Memorial Classes - 1st June 2025

  I would like to thank the committee for inviting me to judge at their show and the exhibitors for their entries, it was a pleasure to have the opportunity to go over your dogs.

  Michael Winch Yearling Award (12,4abs)

 1st Derry, Schoneville and William's Hawklawn Hazzard County at Balvenie JW, 20 month old Tri Dog. A lovely balanced, masculine dog, with lovely head and expression. Super angulations fore and aft, which enabled him to power round the ring with drive with a slashing tail. Lovely tight feet and well off for bone. A very promising young dog, a real showman, who I shall follow his career with interest.

2nd Welburn's Oshowa Just a Dream, 14 month old Blue Bitch. Feminine head, with melting expression and dark eyes. Excellent front and rear angulations, which allowed her to move freely and true. Another who shows lots of promise.

3rd Wilkinson-Sargeant's Walshaw Can't Stop Dancing

Lesley Allan-Scott Open Award (8,1abs)

1st Dyke's Caleydene Connextion, 4 year old orange bitch. Very pretty head, with a soft and gentle expression. Long lean neck, leading to well angulated shoulders. Excellent rear angulation with stifles well bent, which allowed her to glide round the ring with ease, holding her level topline. Lovely deep chest and spring of rib. She is in super coat and condition.

2nd Deville's Dutch CH Fishwick Play it Cool, 3 year old tri bitch. Another lovely girl, slightly larger frame than 1 but still feminine, with excellent balance overall. Lovely head shape with well defined stop, and super dark eyes. Moved with drive with slashing level tail action. She just needs more coat to complete the overall picture.

3rd Wilkinson-Sargeant's Phenset Firestorm

Judge: Emily Bennet (Caprow)
