Southern English Setter Society open show13th July 2014

Judge: Mrs Anne Derry (Balvenie)

A big thank you to officers and committee for the wonderful hospitality extended to me at this lovely breed club open show. I really enjoyed my day and thank exhibitors for their courtesy and sporting acceptance of my decisions.

Best in show was the bitch Culverwell Calamity Jane, Reserve Best in Show was the dog Bramstorm Walk the Line, Best Puppy in Show was the dog Mandysett Leave it to Lewis at Halliana and the Best Veteran was the dog Sh Ch Fishwick Dancing Lad

Mpd(0)

Pd (1)

1 Harradine's Mandysett Leave it to Lewis at Halliana . Nearly twelve month old tri dog, just right for his age. Clean outline, good reach of neck into well laid back shoulders. Masculine head, but not over done, low set ears and lovely dark eyes. Well off for bone, well ribbed up and plenty of body. Good rear angulation. When settled he moved well, using his tail and maintaining his top line. Feet just need to tighten. Best puppy dog and Best puppy in show

Vd (2)

1 Wright's Sh Ch Fishwick Dancing Lad, 8 year old darker orange presenting a balanced outline and in good coat and condition. Masculine head with dark eyes, reachy neck into good layback of shoulders. Short coupled and with good rear angulation. Strode out well, maintaining top line on the move. Enjoyed his day out and was happy to award him best veteran in show.

2 Dunks Samelen one Man, lighter orange of nearly 9years. Another with balanced outline, though slightly longer cast than 1. Masculine head and good reach of neck into well laid back shoulders. Not quite the rear angulation of the class winner. In good coat and condition. Moved with drive, but not just as true as 1 in front.

Class4 Junior dog (4 , 1 absent)

1 Mellish's Walshaw Blinded by Love for Monbrec, blue dog of 13months with lots to like. Smaller mould but still masculine. Correct head, with lovely dark eye and low set ears. Well laid back shoulders. Well off for bone and body, well ribbed up. Short coupled, with good rear angulation. Moved well, once he settled

2 Bennett's Mariglen Blue Star, 17 month old blue. Slightly longer cast and not quite as together as the winner. Masculine head, but just lacking in stop at the moment. Well laid back shoulders and OK for bone. Rear angulation not quite as good as 1, but did move with drive. Just needs time to come together.

Michael Winch Yearling Memorial Award (8,2)

1. Kirsch's Sorbus Hugo Boss. Orange belton dog of 21 months. Pleasing lean head with dark eyes . Clean outline with good length of neck, straight front, and level top line which he held on the move. Well-boned and muscled, very well presented and in good coat.

2. Oliver's Wansleydale Faerie Queen at Bramstorm. Promising 8 months orange bitch, with lovely outline when stood. Pretty head and expression from dark eyes. Well-angulated, well-sprung ribs and good tail set. Neat feet. Really liked her but she was giving her handler a hard time today.

3. Hodge's Lakecastle Tomo

Lesley Allan-Scott Open Memorial Award

1. Whittington's Juldeane Excalibur ShCM. Blue belton and tan dog with pleasing masculine head and dark eye. Presented a clean outline with straight front, level topline, ribs leading well back and correct tailset. Moved true and with style.

2. Oliver's Bramstorm Walk the Line. Substantial dark blue belton and tan dog with masculine head and expression. Strong round bone, short-backed and well ribbed. Well-angulated fore and aft. Moved steadily with good width behind.

3. Tompsett's Radbrooks Kokomo.
