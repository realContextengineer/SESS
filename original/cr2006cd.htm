Southern English Setter Society Championship Show 19 November 2006

English Setter (Dogs)

Judge Mr Steve Collier (Roszarke)

The SESS committee ran a well organised show with a friendly atmosphere, making it a very enjoyable show to judge. It was good to see some quality puppies coming through and a depth of quality in the older classes. The principle winners were agreed jointly with my co-judge Mrs Parsons; BIS was the dog Valsett Starlite Shine On, RBIS was the bitch Sh.Ch. Fenston Arabella At Mariglen, and BPIS was the dog Mariglen Pengtsson's Legacy.

Minor Puppy Dog - 5 entries (2 absent)

1. Hammond's Hartsett Going For Gold With Oaktrish - Young puppy with balanced head and pleasing expression. Shown in good condition, well bodied up, with level top line and good tail set. Moved steadily with straight front and good rear drive. 2. Wright's Fishwick Dancing Lad - Not as mature as the winner and needs more time to settle. Lean balanced head with dark eye and soft expression. Excels in good reach of neck and correct lay-back of shoulder. Needs time to tighten on move. 3. Williams Winterpick Wots It About

Puppy Dog - 3 entries

1. Dennis's Mariglen Pengtsson's Legacy - Outstanding young blue dog, showing a great deal of promise, elegant in appearance, balanced throughout, short coupled and shown in excellent coat and condition. Masculine head with the right amount of work and dark eye making a very attractive young boy. Good reach of neck with clean shoulders and level top line. Straight front and good rear movement, used his tail well and good head carriage. Pleased to see him go best puppy in show. 2. Blackie's Taggleshaw Royal Ensign Over - Promising puppy that still needs time to mature. Good round bone and balanced outline, balanced head with dark eye making a soft expression. Needs to tighten on move. 3. Thompson's Samelen Jupiter

Veteran Dog - 5 entries (2 absent)

1. Harradine's Halliana Dashing Blue - 10 year old veteran dog, still in good coat and condition, and willing to show for his owner. Good reach of neck with correct lay-back of shoulders, short in back with level top line, and good tail set, making a balanced outline. Good positive movement with slashing tail and high head carriage. Went on to win best veteran in show following referee's decision. 2. Mellish's Ownway's Moving Shadow For Monbrec - Another veteran dog happy to be in the ring and showed well for his owner. Clean over lean neck and well laid back shoulders, level top line and good tail-set. Balanced head with dark eye and soft expression. Good round bone and straight on move, moved steadily. 3. Sayers Cumbersett Raven Rebel of Christter

Junior Dog - 7 entries

1. Tucker's Walshaw Moonstorm - Well balanced dog with good conformation and everything in proportion. Straight in front, tight feet, level top line and strong rear quarters. Masculine lean head with dark eye and soft expression. Steady true movement with happy slashing tail. 2. Neath's Albadora Shadow Boxer - Dark blue dog that didn't catch the eye at first glance, but surprised on closer examination. Excelled in neck and shoulders with correct lean head and dark eye making an appealing expression. Shown in good coat and condition. Good rear quarters, providing overall balance. I preferred the top line and movement of 1 on the day. 3. Cole's Sorbus Looks Could Kill

Special Yearling Dog - 3 entries

1. Gilbert's Quensha To The Moon And Back At Shadowwood - Balanced orange dog well bodied with level top line, strong bone and deep brisket. Straight front moved using his tail well. Good reach of neck and clean over shoulders. 2. Bates Tudorfield Blue Lightening - Shown in good coat and condition with level top line and well set-on tail. Elegant lean head with dark eye. Steady on the move, straight at both front and rear. Would have preferred deeper brisket for more heart room. 3. Lenzi's Pilsdon Jack-A-Dandy

Maiden Dog - 5 entries

1. Tucker's Walshaw Moonstorm - won Junior dog 2. Baker's Mandyset First Offence - A very close decision between 1 and 2. Moved true with purpose and drive. Good round bone, level in top line and good tail set. I preferred the head of 1 on the day. 3. Neath's Albadora Shadow Boxer

Novice Dog - 6 entries

1. Tucker's Walshaw Moonstorm - won Junior and Maiden dog 2. Baker's Mandyset First Offence - 2nd in Maiden dog 3. Cozens Earldoms Bold Forester

Moved freely with natural high head carriage and good driving rear action using his tail correctly. Pleased to award RCC; should go on to become a show champion. 2. McCabe's Rachdale Wind Bird - Mature orange dog I have liked and judged well before and who pushed hard for first place.
