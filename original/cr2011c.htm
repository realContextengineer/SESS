Southern English Setter Society

Championship Show Sunday 20th November 2011

Not a big entry but I had some pleasing exhibits to go over. As a Gundog All-Rounder I do not try to out-do the Breed Specialists but aim for a soundly made dog that conforms to the standard and moves well - I hope to get the heads right if I can. The venue is a great hall - but I was glad of Sat-Nav to find it. It was also pleasant to catch up on old friends from the Elstead Club where I trained for 30 years; we always had a good English Setter contingent attending.

One thing that did strike me and needs mentioning - FEET - no gundog's feet should be flat or with thin pads - of which I saw far too many today. I know most of us do not have the opportunity of using our dogs for the job they were designed for - but that does not let us forget and change the shape of the foot that the standard requires.
