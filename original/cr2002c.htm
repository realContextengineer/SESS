SOUTHERN ENGLISH SETTER SOCIETY CHAMPIONSHIP SHOW

November 17 th 2002

Judge: Mrs Joy Field DOGS:

My thanks to the Officers and Committee for inviting me to judge the dogs at their Championship Show and for their warm hospitality. The new venue provided the benefit of generous sized rings and good lighting. I had many lovely dogs to go over but I have one main concern regarding mouths. The incisors in many cases are so small that with normal wear and tear they are likely to be worn away at a very early age. This fault together with level bites gives cause for concern.

SOUTHERN ENGLISH SETTER SOCIETY CHAMPIONSHIP SHOW

BITCHES

Judge Sue Wilkinson

It was nice judging the breed again but feel that the overall quality is not what it used to be particularly in the puppy classes and this is where the breeding stock of the future lies. The Open Bitch was a tremendous class; the first four could change places any day. The feet on two third of the entry was appalling. No amount of roadwork will ever change bad feet. This is a fallacy. It is in the breeding, so do give this some consideration. Tails are another problem. They should be set almost in line with the back. Many were set on to low detracting from a level topline. There were not to many gay tails but the ones with them were those whose tails were set very low.
