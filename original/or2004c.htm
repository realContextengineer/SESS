JUDGES REPORT - S.E.S.S. OPEN SHOW 18/07/04

Thanks to the officers and committee for making me feel welcome and of course the exhibitors for preparing and showing on the day. The weather was perfect and I very much enjoyed the opportunity to judge your dogs. BIS was Cockett's Hollygrove Liarbility RBIS Halstead's Hammoon Little Nutmeg BOS Barker's Elmwood Glentana BPIS Cocketts Hollygrove Penny Puzzle. BVIS Wright's Wynacre Summer Gold.

DOGS

Puppy (1)

1st Well's Hollygrove Storyteller of Ritanor. Well grown 9 month old blue, very much a baby. Nice shaped head soft expression lovely dark eye. Good reach of neck into good shoulders, correct length of body. Obviously at this age muscles are still developing therefore still very much at the gangly stage, overall nice puppy BPD

Special Veteran Dog 7-9 years (3:2a)

1st Brayshaw's Carganey Gambling Man very young looking eight year old in excellent condition thoroughly enjoyed his run around the ring.

Special Veteran Dog 10+ (1)

1st Wright's Wynacre Summer Gold How good this ten year old orange boy still looks very stylish well muscled for his age and it showed going around the ring. BVIS

Stella Oliver ( Judge )

Michael Winch special yearling memorial class

1 Bryant's Phenset Phoenix Rising. Pretty young lady with pleasing head, good reach of neck and correct angulation especially at rear. Sound on the move. Well presented and handled. 2 Hammond's Hartsett Paper Skye for Oaktrish. Good size boy with well rounded bone. Not as finished as the bitch which is what one would expect at this age.

Lesley Allan-Scott special open memorial class.

1 Halstead's Hammoon Little Nutmeg. Standing presented a balanced picture with that "look at me" air about her which made her stand out in a good mixed class. Nearly threw it away on the move but when settled got it together and deserved her first place. 2 Land's Tattersett Firebird. Not the ring presence of my winner but nicely made all through and didn't let me down on the move. 3 Barker & Godfrey's Elmwood Glentana
