SOUTHERN ENGLISH SETTER SOCIETY OPEN SHOW - JULY 21st 2024

 

Judge MRS. P. HARRIS

 

It was an honour and a pleasure to judge this wonderful breed. My thanks to the Society for the invitation and to all the exhibitors for my entry. On the whole exhibits were presented in wonderful condition, just a few that could maybe lose a few pounds. I was a little concerned with so many high tail carriages when on the move, to me there is nothing better than seeing a setter moving with the typical slashing tail action. Having said that I had a lovely and enjoyable day judging your beautiful dogs. Thank you all for accepted my decisions so sportingly.
