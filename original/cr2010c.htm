SOUTHERN ENGLISH SETTER SOCIETY November 2010 Bitches

I thoroughly enjoyed my day and would like to thank the committee for their kind hospitality and express my appreciation to the exhibitors for their entry and time taken preparing their setters.

This year one large ring enabled exhibitors to show their dogs first followed by a short interval and then the bitches. Although my entry was perhaps small in numbers quality was high. My overall winner, in my opinion, possessed breed type with fitness and finish. I was pleased to see her awarded Best In Show along with my Best Veteran Bitch - Best Veteran in Show and the Best Puppy Bitch - Best Puppy in Show.

Minor Puppy Bitch 1 entry. 1st Dale's Moorbrook Little Mermaid at Laurelset. Pleasing head, good eye, well bodied for age, moved OK.

Puppy Bitch 7 entries. 1st Goutorbe's Upperwood Snowball for Redhara. What a stylish puppy, plenty of work in head darkest of eyes, clean neck and shoulders, short coupled with good width and bend of stifle, an extrovert mover, once settled she carried herself well, high head with good tail action. Best Puppy in Show. 2nd .Sykes Daraquist By Jingo. Good head and eye, reachy neck, well laid shoulders, good bone, spring of rib and body, angulated quarters, shown in good coat, moved soundly. 3rd Tompsett's Radbrooks Kokomo

Veteran Bitch 3 entries

1st Dennis's Sh Ch Fenston Arabella at Mariglen, Lovely bitch, still possesses all her attributes that has made her the Show Champion she is, good head and eye, overall well balanced throughout, moved out well with drive, in good coat and condition a credit to her owner- Best Veteran in Show. 2nd Scott's Halliana Theme for a Dream Among Seashadows Good head, dark eyes, overall pleasing body properties, moved soundly. 3rd Davis's Merleycopse Love of a Lady
