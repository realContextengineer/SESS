JUDGES REPORT - S.E.S.S. OPEN SHOW 15/06/07

 SOUTHERN ENGLISH SETTER SOCIETY

Sunday 15th July 2007

Judge: Mrs Jakki Stubbington (Bondgate)

My thanks to the exhibitors for a quality entry of beautiful dogs and bitches, all of which had consistently good heads, which I believe to be the essence of this breed. Some exhibits did, however, lack in depth of chest and movement varied widely. Sadly, some very well constructed entries left their best coats at home on the day.

I must also thank my stoic stewards, Doreen and Clive and the officers and committee for their kind hospitality and hard work in producing the show. The weather was a bit iffy in the afternoon and not everyone agreed with the decision to move inside for the bitch judging. Thankfully, it cleared up enough for us to move outside for the challenge for BIS etc. BIS Dennis's Mariglen Pengtsson's Legacy. RBIS and BOS Littlechild's Sh Ch Ravensett Bianca. BPIS Collier's Ravensett My Kinda Guy at Roszarke BVIS Dunks' Sh Ch Samelen Shooting Star

MINOR PUPPY DOG (3,2)

1 Collier's Ravensett My Kinda Guy at Roszarke. Striking, well grown o/b 8mths. Very pleasing head and neck which gave him the edge in the challenge for best puppy dog. Well off for bone. Moved v well for a youngster, keeping his outline and topline and so sound, fore and aft. BPD and BPIS. 2 Young's Decoverly Sundance to Brookedge. Close up to 1 but preferred head and bone of 1. BPIS These 2 will probably change places many times.

PUPPY (I)

1 Sykes' Daraquist Down Under Stood alone but a worthy winner. Lovely o/b. Really liked him. Kept his outline on move with good tail action. Beautiful forehand with good bone and nice short pasterns. Good bend of stifle and well let down hocks.

SPECIAL VETERAN DOG 7 - 9 (3)

I would just like to comment that the veteran classes were an absolute joy to judge with some fabulous oldies and all a real credit to their owners to present them in such good order. It filled my heart to see the way these oldies performed, enjoying every minute of it and I felt honoured to see some of my old favourites on the day. Thank you! 1 Barker and Godfrey's Sh Ch Caleydene Endeavour for Elmwood. Very striking still at nearly 9 years of age. Excels in construction with the longest of necks. In good coat. Lively mover, really enjoying his day out 2 Mellish's Ownways Moving Shadow for Monbrec. 8 yrs Another fine example of the breed. Very close decision, just lost out to 1 on movement. 3 Jolley's Bellesett Masterpiece of Jolymore. Withdrawn as a little lame.

SPECIAL VETERAN DOG 10 AND OVER (2, 1)

1. Dunks' Sh Ch Samelen Shooting Star Truly remarkable 10 y/o presented in top condition. A real credit to his owner. Beautiful head and expression. Excellent conformation, still very sound on the move. Would give some of the younger dogs a run for their money. Best Veteran dog and BVIS.

JUNIOR DOG (5, 3)

1.Wright's Fishwick Dancing Lad V pleasing 15 m/o o/b. Good head and expression with nice dark eye. Soundly constructed, presenting good outline. Good forehand, depth of chest, turn of stifle and nice feet. Plenty of bone. Nice coat and good tail action. Very sympathetically handled. 2 Jolley's Wansleydale Loganberry at Jolymore. Another nice youngster, close up but just preferred movement and forehand of 1. 3 Arkley's Hartsett Poetry in Motion.

YEARLING DOG (6,5)

1. Dennis's Mariglen Pengtsson's Legacy BD & BIS Love this elegant young b/b from head to tail. Glorious lean balanced head with dark eye and kind expression. Superb neck into clean shoulders and level topline which he holds very well on move. He is so balanced, well muscled and sound throughout and a very flashy light blue. Beautifully presented. He oozes ring presence which gave him the edge over the bitch in the challenge for BIS. I am sure he will go on to be one of the top winning dogs in the breed with time. 2.Tucker's Walshaw Moonstorm Half brother to 1 and similar type but shorter in neck and longer coupled. 3.Dunk's Samelen One Man.

OPEN (11, 10)

A really nice class full of quality bitches. I was splitting hairs to arrive at my final placings. My decisions revolved around weight, balance and resultant toplines.

1. Littlechild's Sh Ch Ravensett Bianca. Glorious o/b bitch, very well presented in super condition. Typical feminine expression. Good angulation fore and aft. V strong over loin. Moved soundly. BB and RBIS. 2. Oliver's Wansleydale Songbird at Bramstorm. Another gorgeous bitch, tri with v dark eye, well presented. I was splitting hairs over these two. RBB. 3. Morgan's Fishwick Queen of the Night at Quensha.
