Open (4)

I was delighted to have the opportunity to go over these 4 show champions and would like to thank their owners for bringing them. They all have the quality and characteristics that our breed require and I made my decisions based on how they looked on the day.

1. Mr & Mrs J W Watkins SH CH Wansleydale Rowanberry Of Valsett, Quality orange, on top form today. Strong masculine head, compact body, well angulated quarters excels in movement and won this class with his sound driving action. In fabulous coat and condition, shown faultlessly as always by this exhibitor.

Southern English Setter Championship Show

Suntop Memorial Award

Super class of quality Dogs and Bitches. I would have liked more cards!

1st · Ms M Withy and Ms F Mitchell's Sunsprake Raging Storm Over Scratchwood. Fitted my interpretation of a near perfect English Setter dog. Orange Belton dog, with balanced head from nose to occiput. Scissor bite,flews not pendulous, well defined stop, dark gentle eyes. Good reach of neck , well placed shoulders, good length of upper arm. Deep chested and short coupled. Lovely bend of stifle, tight feet and in hard condition and good silky coat. Moved well, with level topline and correctly held tail. Beautifully presented and handled.

2nd · Mr G &Mrs K & Miss Z Stirk's Ravensett Diamond Romance With Grakar ScCM Elegant tri bitch. Prettily marked fine head. Dark mischievous eyes, long neck into well laid shoulders. Good length of upper arm, deep chested. Level topline held on the move, good muscletone all over. Moved with vigour, but handled and presented to perfection

3rd · Mr W & Mrs D Bridgewater& Mrs R Davies &Mr B Jeffery's Brucelm Diamond Harry Rocks Bridgella

Judge RITA ALDRIDGE
