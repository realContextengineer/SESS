How to get there

The Venue is at the Chievely Village Hall at the west end of Chievely Village.

From the junction of the M4 Junction 13 and the A34 Newbury to Oxford Road. Take the A34 Northbound from the Roundabout and then the first exit off the A34 to Cheively. At the top of the off ramp turn left towards Chievely Village.

From the A34 the show is approx 1 mile.

The Junction at the A34/M4 is at present being rebuilt so look out for local diversions and lane changes.
