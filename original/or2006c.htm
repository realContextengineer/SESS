JUDGES REPORT - S.E.S.S. OPEN SHOW 16/06/06

 Southern English Setter Society Open Show at Worplesdon, Nr Guildford, Surrey on Sunday 16th July 2006.

It was a pleasure to judge the Southern English Setter Open Show held at Worplesdon. The weather really was too hot to show dogs, which reflected in the number of absentees, so I fully understand why exhibitors wanted to keep their dogs at home. Nevertheless, I want to thank all exhibitors and dogs that did come along and the Officers and Committee who rounded off what was for me a most enjoyable day especially by providing some shade in the ring for myself and the dogs. BIS Day's Decoverly Celtic Venture, RBIS Gilbert's Mariglen Steals the Show at Shadowood BPIS Dunks' Samelen One Man, BVIS Young's Ownways Touch of Spice.

Minor Puppy Dog (4, 2a)

1. Taylor and Atyeo's Samelen Saturn and 2. Thompson's Samelen Jupiter. Two raw 6 mth old litter brothers, both well made with gentle expressions and dark eye, just preferred the steadier movement of 1. Both need lots of time to grow into their legs.

SOUTHERN ENGLISH SETTER SOCIETY

OPEN SHOW - SUNDAY 16 JULY 2006

My thanks to all exhibitors and dogs for turning out on this exceptionally hot day, as by these midday classes, many were really feeling the heat. I had two lovely classes and enjoyed my appointment.

MICHAEL WINCH SPECIAL YEARLING MEMORIAL CLASS - 7 (3abs)

1) Croft's Ravensett Dark 'N' Delicious with Thistlesett - Dark Blue Bitch, 20 months old. Sweet head, with soft expression. Good reach of neck, shoulders well laid back and good forearm angulation. Short coupled with well bent stifle, tight feet. Excellent coat and condition. After an erratic start, moved well, with slashing tail and keeping level topline. 2) Dunks' Samelen One Man - Promising orange dog 10 months old. Stunning head, already showing work, dark eyes. Correct shoulders. Short coupled with deep body, super round bone and neat feet. Steady and happy mover. 3) Taylor & Atyeo's Samelen Saturn.

LESLEY ALLAN-SCOTT OPEN MEMORIAL CLASS - 18 (8 abs)

1) Holland's Mandyset Dirty Dancing - Elegant Blue boy that has matured nicely. Masculine head, with dark eye, low earset and good length of neck into well placed shoulders. Tight feet, good depth of body, superb sweep of stifle with plenty of muscle. In full coat and excellent presentation. Moved with enthusiasm here, which won him the class. 2) Day's Decoverley Celtic Venure - Well constructed Blue Belton and Tan, nice head with well defined stop and dark eye, straight front, excellent spring of rib and plenty of bone, good bend of stifle. Moved O.K. but seemed to be feeling the heat by this time. Good coat and condition. 3) Young's Money Talks .

Cheryl Collins (Judge)
