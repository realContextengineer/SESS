Southern English Setter Society – 21st November 2009

DOGS

I was very pleased with the quality of dogs here today. The younger classes whilst not large, held some very nice, very promising dogs which can only bode well for the breed. BIS was Wansleydale Rowanberry of Valsett, RBIS Richecca Paper Doll with Upperwood and BPIS Redhara Crystal Cracker.

Minor Puppy 3 (1). 1.Littlechild’ Ravensett Armani. Very smart o/b of good size, classic head with lovely head and dark eyes, short coupled, very good tailset and carriage, good hind movement, a little loose in front but just right for age. 2.Hill’s Mandyset Slippery Customer. 6 month o/b with lovely head, showing a lot of promise and moving nicely.

Puppy 2 1.Oliver’s Bramstorm Walk The Line. Dark b/b/t of correct size, nice head and expression good reach of neck, nice outline with really well angulated hindquarters, moved well to win this class. 2.Golby & Martin’s Sunhouse Here Comes The Sun to Shadymoore. Promising dark o/b with very nice head, strong bone, level topline, moved well behind , still a little erratic in front but this will change with age, not as forward as 1 but a lot to like about him.

Veteran 6 (2). 1.Hammond’s Bournehouse Paper Parade for Oaktrish. 9 years old with best outline in this class, b/b/t with nice head and soft expression, straight front, a little heavy over the shoulder but with deep body, good quarters, very happy on the move. 2.Holland’s Mandyset Dirty Dancing. 8 years b/b in lovely coat, straight front with good length of upper arm, nicely angulated, moved well but not the drive of 1. 3.Jolley’s Dyrham Dont Stop Me Now In Jolymore

Junior 6 (3). 1.Swannell’s Rickory Up Close `N` Personal. Promising & very nice b/b of good size and substance, elegant outline, lovely reach of neck, deep body and strong quarter, good tailset and in good condition and well coated, moved well. 2.Mitchell & Withey’s Sunsprake Raging Storm over Scratchwood. O/b I liked a great deal but who is very much at the teenage stage and now needs to deepen in body and grow his adult coat, another who is very promising and when he matures should do really well. 3.Hutchinson’s Walshaw Midnight Moonlight

Yearling 5 (1). 1.Sayer’s Mariglen Blue Flame for Christter b/b with lovely head, clean & elegant outline, long reach of neck into well laid shoulders, straight front, level topline with well angulated front and hind quarters, excellent tailset, free, easy mover. A very nice young dog. 2.Grimsdell’s Tattersett Doctor Woody JW, b/b and very close up to 1, larger framed dog with super depth of body, good bone & tight feet, very nice head, excellent angulation, in very hard condition, in profuse coat, moved well behind but was a little erratic today in front movement. 3.Wood’s Swannery Magical Moment

Maiden 1. 1.Salmon’s Burleycott Irish Cream. O/b with nice head & eyes, straight front, in nice condition, happy on the move.

Novice 3 (2). 1.Hutchinson’s Walshaw Midnight Moonlight. b/b of nice type, lovely head, straight front, tight feet, in hard condition, sound mover, just needs more body.

Tyro 3 1.Ball & Morgan’s Gemsett I Am Legent, elegant b/b with nice head, arched neck of correct length into good shoulders, correct outline, strong quarters, nice front and good bone, best mover in this class. 2.Full’s Daraquist Leading Partner, good size o/b with lovely expression, well off for bone and body and in good condition, wouldn’t settle on the move but has a lovely tail action. 3.Swannery Magical Moment

Graduate 7 (4). 1.Loakes’ Goldbirch Guys N Dolls JW, o/b of good size and substance, nice head and expression, straight front, level topline with strong, angulated hindquarters, lovely coat and condition and sound on the move 2.Darley’s Severnsett Mark of Zorro very flashy b/b, correct head and dark eye, well off for bone, deep body, beautifully presented in lovely coat, moved well when settled, close up to 1. 3.Full’s Daraquist Leading Partner

Post Graduate 9 (3). 1.Sykes’ Daraquist Down Under beautiful, compact dark o/b with most lovely head and appealing expression, strong neck into well laid shoulders, he has a level back, is well ribbed up and with good depth of body, an extrovert he moved really well using his hocks and holding his topline, in hard condition. Res cc. 2.Stephenson’s Wansleydale Huckleberry at Steeplow, dark b/b/t and another who I liked very much, slightly heavier than 1 but still a very balanced young dog , good head, correct topline and rib and scores highly in rear angulation with good tailset and carriage, moved well. 3.Fisher’s Beechanger Wagtail JW

Limit 7. 1.Wright’s Fishwick Dancing Lad, top class o/b of excellent type, size and balance. Loved his head and expression, straight front with tight elbows and good length of upper arm, good bone and spring of rib, level back, good bend of stifle, in lovely coat & condition, moved soundly here but unfortunately did not put in any effort in the challenge. Am sure will obtain his title if his enthusiasm returns. 2.Goutorbe’s Redhara Bobby Dazzler JW, dark b/b/t with lovely outline, dark eye with gentle expression, has good neck & shoulders, short coupled, strong topline with deep, well ribbed body, well angulated, tended to move a little wide behind today but in profile is very impressive. 3.Golby’s Shadymoore Cowboys N Kisses

Open 12 (2) A difficult class with some dogs who looked very good standing just not performing on the move 1.Watkins’ Wansleydale Rowanberry of Valsett, glamorous o/b presented in top order, a very balanced dog with correct head, arched, well muscled neck, deep chest with good bone and feet, very good spring of rib, level back and excellent hindquarters, liked him very much standing but felt that on the move he really came into his own covering the ground with strength and precision with a constant wagging tail, CC & BIS .2.Stubbington & Buckley’s Sh Ch Bondgate Single Malt, o/b dog with lovely head and expression, long neck, straight front with good bone, level back, well angulated front and rear, scored over 1 in shoulder, sound on the move but today unlucky to meet 1 in such form. 3.Mombaers’ Ch Ballykissangels Electrode

Special Beginners 5 (2). 1.Atyeo’s Harvencourt Nice Touch, b/b/t boy with nice head and expression, good neck & shoulder, straight front and good hindquarters, moved soundly. 2.Mellish’s Walshaw Christmas Spirit at Monbrec, o/b of good size & substance, good arch to neck, in nice coat and good condition, in his teenage stage at the moment.

Karen Barker

THE SOUTHERN ENGLISH SETTER SOCIETY

BREED CHAMPIONSHIP SHOW

  SESS CHAMPIONSHIP SHOW 21st November 2009 :

 Bitches

I would like to thank the committee for a lovely day and the ladies in the kitchen for a superb meal.

Minor Puppy Bitch 3 (1 abs). 1. Cozens’s Mandyset Blue Murder B/B : 6 ½ months very pretty headed and sweetest expression. Lovely dark eye and correct shape. Straight front into well laid shoulders, deep body, short coupled, tail never stopped wagging. Lovely coat and condition. Needs to settle on the move but first time out and full of herself. 2. Seawards Mandyset Aint Misbehavin'. Tri. Litter sister to 1, same remarks apply. Longer cast than one and not so sure of herself.

Puppy Bitch 3. 1. Goutorbe’s Redara Crystal Cracker : Tri. What a cracker, sweet head, dark eye. Straight front into well laid shoulders. Good body, deep chest. Have handler a hard time, moved soundly with slashing tail. Best Puppy Bitch and B.P.I.S in agreement with co-judge. 2. Oliver’s Line Dancer B/B. Dark blue, gorgeous head, dark eye. Straight front to good shoulder placement, not the body of 1 and longer cast. Gave her handler a hard time on the move. 3. Green’s Tattersett Honeysuckle

Veteran 7 (4abs). What a trio of lovely old ladies enjoying their day out. 1. Dunk’s Samelan Star Bright B/B 9 ½ yrs old. Beautiful headed bitch with the kindest eye and expression. Excellent front, neck and shoulder placement. Well ribbed and good body. Kept her topline on the move and one of the best movers of the day with a lashing tail. A credit to her owner. Best Veteran in Show, in agreement with my co-judge. 2. Davis Merleycopse Love of a Lady. B/B Sweet head, dark eye. Good body, longer cast than 1. Moved well. 3. Greenham’s Lewkins Wideload Winnie at Frizzanti.

Junior 7 (2 abs). 1. Watkins Valsett Starlite Rosie Moon O/B. Well chiselled head and lovely dark eye. Straight front to well laid shoulder, good bone. Deep body, well ribbed. Good bend of stifle. Moved with drive. Immaculately presented in good coat and condition. 2. Mitchell amd Withey’s Sunsprake Summer Breeze O/B. Another lovely bitch who could change places on another day. Sweet head, dark eye. Good neck and shoulders, well ribbed. Moved soundly. Just left her coat behind but this did not detract from her lovely shape. 3. Tucker’s Walshaw Moonlight and Roses

Yearling 6 (2abs). 1. Baker’s Mandyset Pop Tart. Tri. Very dark tri, beautiful headed bitch with correct eye shape and colour. Well off for bone. Excellent reach of neck into well laid shoulders. Well ribbed deep body. My only criticism feet could be tighter. Moved with drive and style. 2. Aryeo’s Pewterspeare Prying Eyes Pale Tri.Stronger in head than 1 but still feminine. Deep chest, well ribbed and bodied. Good bend of stifle. Lovely tail action on the move. 3. Neath’s Albadora Miss Quizadora

Maiden 2. 1. Goutorbe’s Redara Crystal Cracker . 2. Aryeo’s Pewterspeare Prying Eyes

Novice 4 (1 abs). 1. Goutorbe’s Redara Crystal Cracker 2. Aryeo’s Pewterspeare Prying Eyes .3. Neath’s Albadora Miss Quizadora

Tyro 2 (1 abs). 1. Tucker’s Walshaw Moonlight & Roses ; pretty B/B 3rd in a strong junior class. Sweet head, good eye colour and shape. Needs to body up but plenty to like about her. Moved well.

Graduate (1) 1. Carlin’s Wynacre Last Tango for Sodalitas O/B. Stood alone but a worthy winner. Well chisled head, sweet expression. Excellent body and well bent stifles. Moved soundly with lovely tail action.

Post Graduate 12 (3 abs)/. 1. Attwood’s Swannery Finlandia O/B. Sweetest of heads, soft melting expression. Correct upper arm and lay back of shoulders. Well ribbed and carrying the correct weight. Well bent stifles. Balanced picture standing and moved well. 2. Henry’s Beechanger Little Shrew Tri. Well chisled head. Good bone, eell ribbed. Played her handler up but eventually settled and moved soundly. 3. Baker’s Mandyset What a Diamond.

Limit 10 (1 abs). A lovely class of quality bitches.1. Goutorbe’s Richecca Paper Doll with Upperwood. O/B A bitch I have admired from the ringside and I was not disappointed on going over her. A head to die for, well chiselled, dark eye, soft kind expression. Long neck into well laid shoulders. Well ribbed and carrying correct weight. Well bent stifles. A picture standing. Could have done with a bigger ring to do the bitch justice. The one I would dearly loved to take home. C.C. R.B.I.S. 2. Kelly’s Richecca Watercol;ours B/B. Litter sister to 1. Grimsdell’s Tattersett Air of Elegance

Dennis & Fields Sh.Ch. Mariglen Franscesca Fenston O/B.Another lovely headed bitch. Well ribbed and deep chest. Longer cast than one. Immaculately put down as usual from this kennel. Very sound on the move 3. Baker’s Mandyset Only A Caution
