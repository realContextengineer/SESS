Firstly, apologies for taking so long to have the following uploaded to the web having now returned to my secretarial duties.

 I would like to thank all exhibitors for presenting their exhibits in such clean condition but I was perturbed to see some upright shoulders and high carried tails that spoilt the chances of some otherwise good exhibits.

DOGS

I felt privileged to have been invited to judge the dogs at this prestigious show. Mouths on the whole were good but shoulders still cause me concern as does the hind movement action in many dogs which resulted in a few otherwise good dogs been penalised.

Minor Puppy ( 2 0 ) 1 Barlow's Mandysett Mind Over Matter 7 month orange, pleasing head and expression, good body properties for its age, level top line, stifles well bent, quite steady on the move for a youngster. 2 Bates' Tudorfield Goes Forth a six month old orange baby pleasing in outline, good bone and well angulated hindquarters, not as steady as one on the move.

Puppy ( 1 1 ) 1 Montell's Culverwell Flashman although standing alone this 19 month tri was well worth his first placing, attractive head, dark eye, good in front, well sprung ribs and bend of stifle, good coat for a youngster and moved soundly. Best Puppy Dog.

Veteran ( 3 0 ) 1 Jolley's Bellesett Masterpiece of Jolymore nine year old orange shown in good coat, pleasing overall shape, attractive head, straight front and firm rear quarters, moved correctly and pleased, with my co-judge to award him Best Veteran in Show. 2 Mellish's Ownways Moving Shadow For Monbrec a well made blue dog, pleasing in head and expression, well off for bone with strong rear quarters and a good level topline which he maintained on the move. 3 Harradine - Wildquest Samba Spirit of Halliana.

Junior ( 13 5 ) 1 Watkin's Wansleydale Rowanberry of Valsett eye catching orange youngster, compact and attractive in profile, pleasing head, excellent front and well set shoulders, forearms straight and widely sprung ribs, strong rear quarters, moved soundly. 2 Jolly's Wansleydale Loganberry at Jolymore - attractive blue, pleasing head and dark eye, good front, shoulders well set back, short and level back, well ribbed up, good rear quarters, stifles well bent, moved soundly. 3 Wansleydale Huckleberry at Steeplow.

Yearling ( 5 2 ) 1 Golby's Shadymoore Cowboys 'n' Kisses well constructed orange, very good bone and pleasing when viewed in profile, good head, nice reach of neck let into well set shoulders, level topline held well on the move, shown in good coat. 2 Wright's Fishwick Darling Lad very close to 1 and these two oranges could easily change places another day, pleasing head, clean muscular neck, good body properties, strong rear quarters, shown in good coat. 3 Andrews' Llinlow Impressionist.

Maiden ( 5 2 ) 1 Brown's Tattersett Double Barrel unplaced in a good Junior class but a worthy class winner here, nice shape of head in profile, good reach of neck let into well laid shoulders, level topline with tail correctly set almost in line with the back. 2 Taylor Atyeo's Pewterspeare Pirate Cove nicely shaped blue, good front and rear angulation shown in good coat, moved happily. 3 Shadwell's Swannery Dark Secret. Novice ( 5 3 ) 1 Wood's Bournehouse Lord Harry attractive blue, pleasing head and dark eye, very nice shape in outline, strong straight and rounded bone in forequarters, body properties good with widely sprung ribs, wide loins and strong hindquarters, moved soundly. 2 Brown's T D Barrel.

Tyro ( 5 3 ) 1 Lenzi's Pilsdon Jack-A-Dandy orange boy, good overall shape, excellent bone, correct shoulder placement, forearms straight, deep in back ribs, well muscled including strong second thighs, moved soundly. 2 Wood's B L Harry.

Graduate Dog ( 7 1 ) 1 Slack's Ravensett Dark Fire quite a dark blue well built dog, proud head and dark eye, good rounded bone, short pasterns, shoulders set back, well sprung ribs and deep in back ribs, strong muscular rear legs, good bend of stifles allowing the dog to move soundly. 2 Grimsdell's Tattersett Final Edition pleasing tri when seen in profile, good body properties with quality forequarters, well set shoulders, back short and level and well ribbed up, good rear angulation, shown in good coat. 3 Ball's Bondgate Wayward Lad From Beaverhill.

Post Graduate ( 7 1 ) 1 Tucker's Walshaw Moonstorm attractive blue, most pleasing of heads, dark eye and appealing expression, nice shape in profile, good forequarters with correct shoulder placement and well defined hindquarters, stifles well let down, moved soundly, just needs a little more time to reach full maturity. 2 Critchell-Imms' Tattersett Smoke Screen a well made tri, pleasing in head and expression, well rounded bone, shoulders well set back, deep chest and good spring of rib, well presented and moved well. 3 Kirsch's Soul Mate of Sorbus.

Limit ( 11 3 ) 1 Wells' Hollygrove Story Teller At Ritanor attractive blue with the most appealing of heads so well proportioned, expressive dark eyes which complete the picture, very good shape in outline and does not disappoint on examination, good bone, sound forequarters, lean slightly arched neck let into well set back shoulders, body well ribbed up, hindquarters strong and muscular, well coated and moved with style covering the ring effortlessly. Pleased to award him the CC and to have the Referee award him B.O B. 2 Day's Decoverly Celtic Venture a very good compact tri dog pleasing overall shape, attractive head, good dark eye and expression, well placed shoulders, strong body properties and sound rear quarters enabling him to move well with a good driving ation off the hocks. Pleased to award him the R.C.C. 3 Mellish's Harvancourt In Touch With Monbrec.

Open ( 12 4 ) 1 Hill & Littlechild's Sh Ch Ravensett American Gigalo At Somerled dark blue, lovely shape in outline, well proportioned head and good dark eye, strong forquarters, correct shoulder placement, level topline, good bend of stifle, well presented in good coat, lost to the CC winner in the challenge on movement. 2 Derry's Sh Ch Balvenie What A Sensation orange with a pleasing head and expression, well constructed both front and and quarters, good shoulders and top line, not in the best of coats on the day and movement whilst sound lacked his usual sparkle. 3 Andrews' Llinlow Sovereign Gold.

Special Beginners ( 6 1 ) 1 Slack's Ravenset D Fire. 2 Stephenson's Wansleydale H A Steeplow placed third in a strong Junior class - a well made tri, pleasing shape in profile both standing and on the move, straight in front, good shoulder placement, lean muscular neck, strong hindquarters, shown in good coat and moved soundly. 3 Jolley's Dyrham Dont Stop Me Now In Jolymore.

Elwyn Evans

Judge
